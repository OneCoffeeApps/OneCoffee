Keep your Mac awake seamlessly. Control it remotely from your iPhone with real-time Push Notifications.

Download for macOS

How to install the iOS PWA

  1. Download and install OneCoffee on your Mac.
  2. Open the app and scan the QR code with your iPhone.
  3. In Safari, tap the Share button and select "Add to Home Screen".
  4. Open the newly added app on your iPhone and enable Push Alerts!
